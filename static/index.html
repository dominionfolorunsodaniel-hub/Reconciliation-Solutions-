        Privacy Policy
        
      
        Last updated: October 2026
        
      
        1. What we collect
        
      
          
        
        • Account details: your username, company name, an optional Telegram ID, and a securely scrambled (hashed) password. We never store your password in plain text.
          • 
        
        • Your reports and questions: the files you upload and the questions you ask about them.
          • 
        
        • Payment details you send us: the transfer reference and the payment screenshot you attach.
          • 
        
        • Security and usage records: a log of important actions (such as sign-ins and uploads), and your network address so we can block repeated failed sign-ins.
          • 
      
        
      
        2. How we use it
        
      
        To run the service for you: analyzing and reconciling your reports, sending alerts and PDF reports, confirming payments, keeping the service secure, and giving support. We do not sell your data.
        
      
        3. AI processing
        
      
        To answer your questions, relevant parts of your report data are sent to an AI service provider (Google Gemini). Before a report is stored or analyzed, we mask card numbers (keeping only the last four digits) and remove columns such as CVV and PIN. Please avoid uploading data you are not allowed to share.
        
      
        4. How we protect it
        
      
        Reports are stored encrypted, passwords are hashed, each company can only see its own data, two-factor login is available, and actions are logged. No system is perfectly secure, but we take these protections seriously.
        
      
        5. Telegram
        
      
        If you link your Telegram ID, we may send you alerts and files there. When a customer attaches a payment screenshot, our Telegram bot forwards it to our administrators so they can confirm the payment.
        
      
        6. Who else sees it
        
      
        Only the service providers we need to run Report Analyzer (such as hosting, database, AI, Telegram and email services), and authorities if the law requires it.
        
      
        7. Keeping and deleting data
        
      
        We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your reports or your whole account at any time, and we will do so, including from backups in due course.
        
      
        8. Your choices
