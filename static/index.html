📘 Getting started

  1. Upload a CSV, Excel or supported PDF report.
  2. Ask a plain-language question about totals, trends, dates, branches or failed transactions.
  3. Use Reconcile to compare two reports; use Alerts to review possible anomalies.
  4. Export useful findings to PDF and check important figures against your original records.
  5. Open Plan & usage to see your company's shared quota and request a paid plan.

Paid access starts only after an administrator verifies the bank transfer. AI findings can be wrong; validate high-impact financial decisions against source records.
