Why it is hard

Reconciliation is a pain for almost every finance team

Three things make it slow. Report Analyzer is built to remove each one.

Fragmented systems

The switch, the ledger and the bank each give you a different file. We compare them side by side, so you do not have to merge them by hand.

Messy, unstructured data

Different column names, dates and formats are normal. Upload CSV, Excel or PDF and we read them as they are.

Hours of manual work

No more VLOOKUP marathons. Ask in plain English, even Pidgin, and get the answer, the working and an export.

            2. 
        
          2. Split it by (optional): branch, month, bank, terminal, day, channel.
            3. 
        
          3. Only for (optional): a month, a branch, failed transactions, amounts above ₦50,000, credits or debits.
            4. 
        
          4. How many to show (optional): top 10, bottom 5, all.
            5. 
      
          
      
          Example: “total withdrawals by branch for March, top 5” = what (total withdrawals) + split (branch) + only for (March) + how many (top 5).
